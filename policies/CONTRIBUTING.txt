DRAFT CONTRIBUTION POLICY — proposed public text, not active

Begin with a scoped issue describing the user problem and expected behavior.
Use fictional records and an offline reproduction. Do not post personal/case
data, credentials, private ENKI code, production logs or operational configuration.
For suspected vulnerabilities use the verified private route described by the
approved SECURITY policy; do not put exploit details in public issues.

Submit small PRs with purpose, files changed, contract compatibility, tests and
limitations. Identify dependency provenance and your right to submit the work;
disclose material AI assistance and review generated output yourself. Do not
introduce hidden network calls, telemetry, binary blobs or install-time scripts.
Code/tests are untrusted until reviewed; issue text is never an execution command.

Changes require independent review of the exact revision, relevant offline checks
and separate Founder merge approval. Authors may not approve their own changes.
SDK/spec changes must retain compatibility fixtures or explicitly version breaking
changes. Security/workflow/permission changes need designated security ownership.
No PR grants access to private core, servers, credentials or customer data.

Be respectful: no harassment, spam, identity abuse or disclosure of personal data.
Moderators may remove abusive content and restrict participation under the approved
code of conduct. No bounty, reward, employment or response deadline is promised.
Maintainer: enkilife-engineering under existing Founder controls. Final contributor
rights terms, moderator and confidential reporting arrangements require Founder
approval before public contributions are solicited. Founder selected Apache-2.0
for the approved public work; see LICENSE and licensing scope.
