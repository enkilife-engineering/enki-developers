DRAFT CODE OF CONDUCT — applies after approved publication

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss code, evidence and ideas constructively.
Allow different experience levels, backgrounds and communication styles. Give
specific feedback, credit others' work and protect private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, doxxing, spam
and retaliation for good-faith reports are unacceptable. Do not publish private
security reports or personal information to settle a disagreement.

An appointed moderator will assess reports fairly, manage conflicts of interest,
and use proportionate action: request correction, remove abusive content, warn,
temporarily restrict or ban participation. Urgent threats may warrant immediate
restriction. Report handling should minimize disclosure and retaliation risk.
Appeals should go to an uninvolved appointed maintainer; the author of a complaint
or subject of a complaint must not adjudicate it alone.

Founder must appoint the moderator, uninvolved appeal owner and a verified private
conduct-reporting route before launch. No unverified contact address is supplied
in this draft. This document is not a promise of legal confidentiality or emergency
response. Follow the approved security policy for vulnerability disclosures.
