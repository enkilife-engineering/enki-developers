DRAFT PRIVACY / DATA HANDLING — synthetic developer quickstart

The supplied offline sample uses two fictional records. It makes no network calls,
collects no accounts, identifiers or telemetry and reads no external content or
credentials. It does not send data to model providers or connect to ENKI core.
The local fixture grant is test data, not consent/authentication for real access.

Public contributions reveal the author's chosen GitHub identity and submitted
content through GitHub. Do not submit customer records, case files, personal data,
credentials or private logs. Keep vulnerability reports in the verified private
intake once available, with the minimum synthetic evidence necessary.

Before ANY real connector: approve an actual notice covering accountable entity,
contact route, purposes, data categories, legal/consent basis where applicable,
provider destinations, subprocessors, retention/deletion, revocation and user
rights handling. Obtain granular resource-bound authorization; read-only access
still discloses data. No compliance certification is asserted by this draft.
Real connector activation, credentials and retention are not authorized here.
